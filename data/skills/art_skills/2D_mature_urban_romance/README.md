# Mature Urban Romance Anime Style Guide

This style is built specifically for the **Mature Urban Romance anime** genre. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Anime style**: All characters, scenes, and props follow anime as the standard, emphasizing cel shading, clean lines, and dramatic low-key lighting.
- **Modern urban romance setting**: Contemporary urban life scenes, including apartments, offices, cafes, city streets, etc., with no ancient-style/fantasy elements.
- **Cinematic composition**: Compositions emphasize a cinematic feel, with depth-of-field blur, lens optical characteristics, and aerial perspective.
- **Low-saturation cool tones**: The overall palette leans cool, with warm colors used only as accents, highlighting an atmosphere of cool elegance paired with tender sweetness.

## Scope of Application

- AI art generation for mature urban romance subjects (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual creation for anime styles adapted from modern novels
- Visual work requiring clean lines, cel shading, and cinematic composition

## Strictly Prohibited Content

- Photorealistic/photographic/3D-rendered styles
- Highly saturated fluorescent/neon colors
- Ancient-style/fantasy/sci-fi or other styles outside the modern setting
- Suggestive, exposed, see-through, or other inappropriate content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with refined faces and a cool or gentle temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in a modern urban anime style
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, ultra-clear material textures, and an atmosphere that is both realistic and poetic
- Props, decorations, and accessories all styled for a modern urban setting, with fine craftsmanship and an exquisite texture
- Storyboards and video are anime-based, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "Mature Urban Romance Anime" related projects. For other styles (such as photorealistic, ancient-style, fantasy, etc.), please do not use this prompt set.
