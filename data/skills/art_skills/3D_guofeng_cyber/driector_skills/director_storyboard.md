# Storyboard Prompts - 3D Guofeng Cyber - Style-Exclusive Technique

## Scope of Application
This Skill is dedicated to generating storyboard prompts for the **3D Guofeng Cyber (New-Chinese Cyberpunk)** style, with the core being an organic fusion of "Chinese aesthetics as the soul, cyber sci-fi as the form." It is **perfectly suited to both creative directions at once**:
1.  Cyberization of traditional ancient-style scenes (court, garden, wuxia, sect, etc.)
2.  Guofeng cyberization of modern urban scenes (commercial district, office tower, apartment, night market, etc.)
Rejects rigid element splicing, holding firmly to the Chinese aesthetic core.

## Aesthetic Prohibitions (Strictly Avoid at Generation Time - Dual-Scene Adapted Revision)

The following vocabulary/styles must not appear in output prompts:

- No photorealistic/photographic-realism words (e.g., photorealistic, realistic photography)
- No chaotic high-saturation fluorescent colors / disordered neon lacking Chinese color logic / cheap digital feel
- No pure Western cyberpunk / Japanese cyber / pure sci-fi elements without a guofeng core
- No low-precision modeling / rough texture maps / plastic texture / materials without physical properties
- No cartoon / anime / two-dimensional flat style
- No flat design / lacking 3D depth / perspective error / lighting logic error
- No pure Western modern architecture without a guofeng core / pure mechanical architecture without a Chinese structure / rigid splicing of guofeng and cyber elements
- No content that loses the Chinese aesthetic core and merely piles on cyber elements
- No pure Western modern clothing without a Chinese design / pure sci-fi cybernetic bodies without a guofeng core
- No vulgar, chaotic, or disorderly visual elements that conflict with the Chinese aesthetic tone

> Note - **Exception**: Modern 3D rendering techniques (such as ray tracing, volumetric light, global illumination, physical materials) may be used freely; modern urban architecture with a guofeng core, new-Chinese costume, and modern scene carriers may all be used freely — the core requirement is to hold to the fusion baseline of "Chinese aesthetics as the soul, cyber sci-fi as the form."
