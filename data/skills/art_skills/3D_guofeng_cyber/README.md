## 1. Core Style Definition (Underlying Generation Logic)
All art prompts, specifications, and generated content for this style must be strictly limited to:
- **Guofeng Cyber 3D Rendering**: Centered on 3D modeling and rendering technology, deeply fusing traditional Chinese Eastern aesthetics with the cyberpunk sci-fi core. It locally blends Chinese mortise-and-tenon joinery, ink-wash artistic mood, traditional patterns, and ancient-style forms with holographic light, mechanical structures, neon lighting effects, and futuristic tech elements — rejecting any pile-up of elements without a coherent core.
- **3D Ultra-Fine Texture**: 8K high-precision models, 4K ultra-HD PBR physical material rendering, next-gen game-level topology, with metal/fabric/jade/holographic material textures pushed to the max — every detail crisp and every texture clearly discernible.
- **Guofeng Cyber Exclusive Color Palette**: Based on jade green, vermilion red, indigo blue, gilded gold, and ink black, paired with low-saturation Chinese-style neon colors (cinnabar red neon, malachite blue hologram, gilded glow), avoiding fluorescent bleed — elegance and futurism combined.
- **Eastern Cyber Aesthetic Atmosphere**: An ink-wash mood of the real blending with the illusory, layered with cyber lighting; negative-space composition, Chinese symmetrical aesthetics; misty rain-like volumetric light interwoven with holographic projections; poetic space perfectly fused with a futuristic cityscape feel.
- **Guofeng Cyber Character Design**: 3D next-gen ancient-style modeling with classical Eastern bone structure; costumes are hanfu forms modified with cyber-mechanical elements; makeup is elegant, fused with cyber light-effect face paint; hairstyles are refined, paired with mechanical hair accessories — Eastern charm preserved throughout.
- **Top-Tier Cinematic Lighting**: Top-tier UE5/Blender Cycles rendering, volumetric light, ambient occlusion, global illumination, ray tracing, depth-of-field blur, motion blur, LUT color grading, film-grade cinematography language, atmosphere pushed to the max.

### Scope of Application
- AI art generation for Guofeng Cyber 3D subjects (character art, scene concepts, prop design, storyboards, game CG, film posters, etc.)
- Visual creation requiring 3D next-gen texture, Eastern aesthetic core, and cyber sci-fi atmosphere
- Commercial applications such as Guofeng Cyber game art, film/TV concept design, animated shorts, digital collectibles, etc.

### Strictly Prohibited Content
- Photorealistic photography / real-person photo-level realism (unless specifically noted)
- Pure Western cyberpunk / Western fantasy / Gothic / Victorian style, or stiff combinations lacking a Guofeng core
- Modern everyday urban elements / pure sci-fi machinery with no Chinese elements
- Low-precision models / rough topology / blurry textures / clipping / deformed structures
- Over-saturated fluorescent colors / excessive color bleed / heavy digital noise / low-contrast hazy images
- Western-style modeled faces / deformed bodies / exaggerated, incongruous limb proportions that violate classical Eastern bone structure
- Vulgar/exposed clothing that doesn't conform to hanfu forms / costumes with no cultural core piled with random elements
