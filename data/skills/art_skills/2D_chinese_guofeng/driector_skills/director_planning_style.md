# Guofeng Anime Constraints · Guofeng Anime New Chinese-Chic · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Color-Tone System and Overall Mood

- **Tonal base** — The entire piece is grounded in a core palette: moon white (C1 #E8EAF5), jade green (C2 #4A9B8A), vermilion (C3 #C93752), indigo blue (C4 #2B4C7E), golden yellow (C5 #D4AF37), ink black (C6 #1A1A2E), rouge (C7 #A94A5F), ochre (C8 #965E3E), gamboge (C9 #F5E375), gray-purple (C10 #7B6C85). Overall color temperature leans neutral (5000-5600K), saturation medium-high (55-70%), presenting a rich Eastern classical look through a full new Chinese-chic palette
- **Warm-cool narrative contrast** — Warm colors (vermilion C3, golden yellow C5, rouge C7, gamboge C9) act as visual signals for positive emotion/luxury/celebration; cool colors (indigo blue C4, ink black C6, jade green C2) act as visual signals for conflict/severity/solitude. Warm-cool switching should sync with the story arc, not be mixed arbitrarily
- **Warm-cool allocation strategy** — Wuxia severity/mournful-isolation passages use cool tones (indigo blue + ink black + jade green), forbidding vermilion/golden-yellow warm light; xianxia ethereal/girlish-everyday passages use soft warm tones (moon white + rouge + gamboge); court luxury/festive-celebration passages use vermilion + golden-yellow highlights
- **Palette-first principle** — Segment planning must first bind the emotional scene (xianxia ethereal / court luxury / wuxia severity / girlish everyday / moonlit poetic / festive celebration, etc.), then determine the primary + secondary colors and the light-and-shadow scheme, avoiding a mismatch where "the plot is right but the mood color isn't"
- **Forbidden color gamut** — Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors, and strongly digital-looking colors are all incompatible with this style. Hue-shift tolerance ±8°, saturation shift ±10%, brightness shift ±12%

## 2. Light-and-Shadow Scheme System

- **Light and shadow as narrative** — 7 light-and-shadow schemes correspond to different emotional passages; the director-planning stage should fix the light-and-shadow tonal direction at the segment level, not shot by shot
- **Cel-shaded light and shadow as the anchor** — All light-and-shadow schemes must retain a cel-shaded flat-color texture; shadows are deepened using the same color family, black hard shadows are forbidden, and Japanese-style rendering techniques such as volumetric light/depth-of-field blur are allowed
- **Light-source angle** — Front-facing shots default to a 38.5° angled soft light (cel-shaded flat-color texture); side-facing shots may be adjusted as appropriate

| Scheme | Name | Tonal Tendency | Applicable Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Soft-light clarity | Moon-white base + rouge subtle-warm skin light | Girlish everyday, warm and sweet, healing freshness |
| B | Ethereal immortal air | Moon white + jade green + golden-yellow rim light | Xianxia ethereal, otherworldly mood, stunning entrance |
| C | Warm candlelight | Ochre warm-dominant + ink-black dark areas | Nighttime intimacy, warming closeness, lingering warmth |
| D | Cold moonlight glow | Indigo blue + moon-white cool light | Moonlit poetic, longing solitude, tranquil coldness |
| E | Gauze-filtered light | Moon-white base + dappled side light | Boudoir interior, study everyday life, daytime quietude |
| F | Court splendor | Vermilion + golden-yellow highlights + ink-black dark areas | Court luxury, grand celebration, formal solemnity |
| G | Cold severity | Indigo blue + ink black + 38.5° side-cast cold light, no warm colors | Wuxia showdown, mournful isolation, tense atmosphere |

- **Warm-cool light allocation** — Warm light (warm ochre/vermilion/golden yellow) applies to positive-emotion/luxury passages; wuxia severity/mournful passages always use cold light. The director may adjust the warm-cool switch points as the narrative requires
- **Mood-direction mapping** — Each scene's mood direction should map to one of the light-and-shadow schemes above (A-G) to ensure visual consistency

## 3. Texture Direction

- **Cel-shaded flat color as the anchor** — The core of guofeng anime: delicate lines, cel-shaded flat color, Japanese-style anime rendering, smooth color-block gradients; color-block banding/color bleeding are forbidden
- **Delicate brushwork + cinematic texture** — This is the anchor point of the frame's texture. Not realistic photography, not 3D CG, but a fusion of anime Japanese-style rendering and guofeng aesthetics
- **Anime-ized materials** — All costume/makeup/prop materials must be rendered in cel-shaded flat color: silk sheen via smooth color blocks, metal reflections via cold-sheen lines, jade smoothness via internally-lit color blocks. "3D feel" and "photorealistic feel" are forbidden
- **Skin texture** — Pinkish-white base tone, cel-shaded flat color, natural sheen, soft texture, inner-glow feel, translucent radiant skin. Matte/deathly pale/waxy/oily sheen/overexposure are forbidden
- **Anime does not mean flat** — Guofeng anime new Chinese-chic emphasizes adding spatial and cinematic sense atop the cel-shaded flat-color base through light-and-shadow layering, depth-of-field blur, volumetric light, and other means, rather than a flat, depthless cartoon style

## 4. Guofeng Anime Scene Spatial Elements

Scene elements unique to the guofeng worldview and their visual-narrative function:

- **Gauze curtains/screens/door frames/carved window lattices** — Natural framing-composition props that create a "can't see through" sense of layering and spatial depth, rendered with cel-shaded lines showing exquisite carved detail
- **Courtyard/flowering trees/curtains of rain/blue-green landscape** — Natural vehicles for negative-space composition; the scenery is the emotion: a courtyard full of blooms = relief, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, falling leaves drifting = parting sorrow, misty clouds = ethereal immortal mood
- **Candlelight/moonlight/window light/lanterns** — Light-source vehicles of the guofeng world; candlelight = warm/intimate (Scheme C), moonlight = cold/solitary (Scheme D), window light = everyday/quiet (Scheme E), lanterns = festive/bustling (Scheme F)
- **Upturned eaves and brackets/vermilion palace walls/stone arch bridges/classical furniture** — Architectural elements rendered in new Chinese-chic aesthetics, rich in detail with clean cel-shaded lines; materials carry natural wear/patina of time, a pristine "plastic feel" is forbidden
- **Use scenic cutaways for segment transitions** — This style has abundant scene assets (variants by time of day/weather/shot size); segment transitions should use scenic cutaways as an emotional buffer rather than a hard cut
- **Use visuals rather than dialogue for turning points** — Prefer visual means (sudden light-and-shadow shifts, jump cuts in shot size, symbolic cutaways) over dialogue explanation
- **Foreground/midground/background layering** — Every scene must have foreground/midground/background; flatness is forbidden, and outdoor scenes must include aerial perspective (distant mountains hazy blue-gray)

## 5. Classical Instruments and Ambient Sound

Sound-element constraints under the classical-fantasy worldview:

### Instrument Choice

- **Xiao (vertical flute)** — The core instrument for desolate, solitary, mournful passages, highly suited to moonlit-poetic/xianxia-ethereal moods
- **Erhu** — For emotionally turbulent, grief-stricken, longing passages; its keening bowed tone suits emotional outbursts
- **Suona** — For passages with intense emotional swings (great sorrow and joy, fateful turning points, climaxes); use sparingly but it lands like a bombshell
- **Guqin** — For opening tone-setting / calm passages / xianxia ambiance, often paired with the xiao
- **Pipa** — An accent for tense, urgent passages, a rhythm-driver for wuxia showdown scenes, not the lead
- Strings as an undertone can add cinematic quality but should not overwhelm

### Instrument Combination Strategy

| Emotional Stage | Instrument Combination |
|---|---|
| Calm/opening/closing | Solo guqin, or guqin + xiao |
| Xianxia/ethereal/poetic mood | Xiao + guqin + light bells |
| Deepening sorrow | Xiao + erhu |
| Emotional outburst/fateful turn | Solo suona, or suona + erhu |
| Tense/urgent/wuxia | Pipa accent + drumbeats + string undertone |
| Girlish everyday/sweetness | Guzheng + bamboo flute |
| Court/celebration/grandeur | Bianzhong (chime bells) + guzheng + string undertone |

### Classical Ambient Sound

- **Typical ambient-sound layers** — Cicadas and insects chirping / a babbling stream / wind through bamboo / market street cries / night rain on the eaves / rustling fabric / a soft wind-chime ring / falling petals drifting / the faint glow-sound of fireflies
- **Label 1-2 core ambient sounds per scene** to guide subsequent sound design. The richer the ambient-sound layering, the more immersive the guofeng scene
