# Storyboard-Table Guofeng Anime Constraints · Guofeng Anime New Chinese-Chic · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Positioning of the Storyboard Table

The storyboard table is the director's core tool for converting a script into shot language. The following provides only the guofeng anime new Chinese-chic style constraints at the storyboard-table level.

---

## 2. Light and Shadow, and Mood

- **Unified cel-shaded light and shadow** — Within a single scene, the light-and-shadow scheme must maintain cel-shaded flat-color consistency, with shadows deepened using the same color family (black hard shadows are forbidden), unless there is a clear narrative turning point (such as candlelight dying out → cold moonlight glow)
- **Emotional palette binding** — Each scene must bind at least 1 emotional setting (e.g. xianxia ethereal / court luxury / wuxia severity / girlish everyday / moonlit poetic / festive celebration), and maintain primary+secondary color continuity within the shot group, with colors drawn from the core palette (moon white #E8EAF5 / jade green #4A9B8A / vermilion #C93752 / indigo blue #2B4C7E / golden yellow #D4AF37 / ink black #1A1A2E / rouge #A94A5F / ochre #965E3E / gamboge #F5E375 / gray-purple #7B6C85)
- **Light-source angle specification** — Front-facing shots default to a 38.5° angled soft light source (cel-shaded flat-color texture); side-facing shots are adjusted as the frame requires. When describing light and shadow, the angle difference between front-facing and side-facing must be distinguished, and the light-and-shadow rendering must always retain a Japanese-style anime texture
- **Warm-cool tone matched to narrative stage** — Wuxia severity/sorrowful-mournful passages use cool tones (indigo blue + ink black + jade green), forbidding warm orange/vermilion warm light; xianxia ethereal/girlish-everyday passages use soft warm tones (moon white + rouge + gamboge); court-luxury passages use vermilion + golden-yellow highlights
- **Light-and-shadow transitions are an advanced technique** — A gradual shift from gauze-filtered light to warm candlelight = the passage of time from day to night. Mark light-and-shadow change points in the storyboard table; during the transition, cel-shaded color blocks must blend smoothly, with color-block banding/color bleeding forbidden
- **Japanese-style rendering light effects** — Reasonable use of volumetric light, depth-of-field blur, rim light, and other modern rendering techniques is allowed, but the guofeng anime cel-shaded flat-color aesthetic tone must be preserved; 3D-realistic/CG-animation light effects are forbidden

---

## 3. Environmental Dynamics

- **Environmental dynamics add a sense of breathing to the frame** — Falling petals, swirling mist, rippling water, fluttering gauze curtains, flowing robes. At least one shot with environmental dynamics should be arranged every 3-4 shots, to avoid the frame feeling "dead"
- **Prioritize guofeng-world environmental elements** — Environmental dynamics should draw on elements within the guofeng worldview: falling flowers, drifting catkins, wisps of smoke, flowing water, wind through bamboo, flickering candlelight, drifting clouds and mist, glowing firefly points, drifting snowflakes; modern elements are forbidden
- **Cel-shaded dynamic texture** — Environmental dynamic elements (petals/smoke/water ripples, etc.) must retain a cel-shaded flat-color texture and delicate lines; 3D particle effects or realistic fluid effects are forbidden

---

## 4. Guofeng Anime Action Rhythm

- **Movement is elegant with an animated feel** — Character movement defaults to slow and graceful, but may receive anime-style exaggeration on key frames (such as sharp lines in a wuxia showdown, or flowing hair strands as a girl glances back); rising, turning, and raising a hand should be marked "slow/graceful"
- **Costume dynamics are a core visual asset** — The movement of guofeng anime costume (wide sleeves, skirt hems, shawls, streaming ribbons) is a natural dynamic asset; frame descriptions may add the motion of robes/skirt hems/ribbons, and costume movement under cel-shaded rendering should have clean lines and clear layering
- **Bearing constraints** — A guofeng character's demeanor should match a classical temperament: walking should be steady and unhurried, gestures should be restrained and understated, and modern body language (such as shrugging, palms-up gestures) must not appear. Anime characters may exaggerate slightly in micro-expressions (crescent-moon eyes, flushed cheeks), but body movement must remain classically restrained
- **Consistent head-to-body ratio** — In action design, characters must maintain the classical anime proportion of 6-7 heads tall; dynamic poses must not break this head-to-body ratio

---

## 5. Material and Texture Constraints

- **Cel-shaded flat color as the anchor** — In every shot, the rendering of characters/costumes/props/scenery must retain a cel-shaded flat-color texture, with delicate clean lines and smooth color blocks; 3D-realistic/CG-animation/photorealistic rendering is forbidden
- **Costume material** — The texture of silk + embroidery + pearlescent fabric is rendered via cel-shaded flat color, with ultra-clear texture and a clear fabric feel
- **Prop material** — Cold metallic sheen, translucent jade, clear wood grain, warm smooth porcelain — all rendered via cel-shaded flat color + delicate brushwork; realistic material rendering is forbidden
- **Scene material** — Wear marks on wood surfaces, weathering on stone surfaces, natural creases in fabric — materials carry a sense of age but are rendered with a guofeng anime brushwork; a pristine "plastic feel" is forbidden

---

## 6. Color Constraints

- **Core palette locked** — All shots in the storyboard table must draw color from the core palette (C1-C10); hue-shift tolerance ±8°, saturation shift ±10%, brightness shift ±12%
- **No fluorescent colors** — Highly saturated fluorescent colors/neon colors/strongly digital-looking colors are strictly forbidden
- **Line color** — Ink black or deep brown; pure-black thick lines are forbidden
- **Shadow color** — Deepened using the same color family; black hard shadows are forbidden
- **Overall color temperature** — Leans neutral, a 5000-5600K natural-light base tone, contrast 45-65%, saturation 55-70% (a full new Chinese-chic palette)

---

## 7. Camera-Move Taboos

- **No fast camera moves** — Whip pans, quick push-ins, and handheld shake conflict with the elegant temperament of guofeng anime new Chinese-chic; wuxia showdown scenes may moderately increase the camera-move pace while still keeping the image clear
- **No fancy transitions** — Wipes, spins, venetian blinds, etc. are incompatible with this style
- **No realistic camera language** — Handheld shake/documentary-style tracking/fisheye lenses, etc. conflict with the anime aesthetic and undermine the cel-shaded flat-color purity of the image
