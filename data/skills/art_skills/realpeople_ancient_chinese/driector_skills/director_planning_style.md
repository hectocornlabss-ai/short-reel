# Ancient-Style Constraints · Photorealistic Ancient Chinese · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Color Palette System and Overall Tone

- **Palette base** — The entire piece is grounded in moon white (C1), cool white skin tone (C2), and slate blue-black (C6) as the base colors, with an overall cool color temperature (4800-5200K) and mid-to-low saturation (30-50%), conveying a melancholic, cool, refined gray tone
- **Warm/cool narrative contrast** — Warm colors (amber warmth C7, pearl gold C3, sunset-glow pink C5) serve as the visual signal for narrative turning points, used as local accents in emotionally warming passages. Warm/cool switching should sync with the story arc, not be mixed arbitrarily
- **Warm/cool allocation strategy** — Sorrowful/isolated/oppressive passages must strictly maintain the cool base tone, with warm tones such as warm orange and amber prohibited; warm tones are released only after the emotion warms, using warm/cool contrast to mark narrative turning points
- **Palette-first principle** — Sequence planning should first bind the emotional scene (first meeting/warming up/misunderstanding/reunion/grand wedding, etc.), then determine the primary + accent colors and lighting scheme, avoiding "the plot is right but the emotional color isn't"
- **Forbidden color gamut** — Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors, and modern color systems are all incompatible with this style

---

## 2. Lighting Scheme System

- **Lighting is narrative** — 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; the director's planning stage should determine the lighting-tone direction at the sequence level, not shot by shot
- **Light source angle** — Default 38.5° oblique light for front-facing shots (not 45°); side-lit shots may be adjusted as needed

| Scheme | Scheme Name | Tone Tendency | Applicable Emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Pearlescent soft diffusion | Cool white base + faint warm skin glow | Everyday warmth, relaxed sweetness |
| B | Side-rim ethereal glow | Moon white + pearl-gold rim light | Ethereal appearance, stunning entrance |
| C | Candlelit warm shadow | Amber warmth dominant + ink-jade black shadows | Nighttime intimacy, warming closeness |
| D | Cold moonlit glow | Slate blue-black + frost-snow silver | Nighttime solitude, longing alone |
| E | Gauze-filtered light | Cool white base + mottled side light | Indoor daytime, everyday living |
| F | Diffused skylight | Slate blue-black distance + moon-white haze | Distant view, mist, atmospheric empty shots |
| G | Cold side-light | Slate blue-black cool white + 38.5° side-lit cold light, no warm tones | Sorrowful isolation, oppressive coldness |

- **Warm/cool light allocation** — Warm light (amber warmth/warm orange) applies to emotionally warming passages; sorrowful/isolated passages use cold light without exception. The director may adjust the warm/cool switching point according to narrative needs
- **Mood-direction mapping** — Each scene's mood direction should map to one of the lighting schemes (A-G) above, ensuring visual consistency

## 3. Texture Direction

- **Ultra-clear documentary feel** — The core of photorealistic ancient-style photography: visible pores, individually distinct hair strands, ultra-clear texture detail
- **Strong contrast + extreme detail** — This is the anchor of the visual texture. Not film grain, not ink-wash impressionism — this is cinematic-grade documentary photography
- **Material realism** — All costume, makeup, and prop materials must be believable: the sheen of silk, the texture of cotton and linen, the reflectivity of metal, the smoothness of jade. "Plastic feel" and "CG feel" are prohibited
- **Skin texture** — Real skin pores, subtle imperfections, and sheen. Neither overly smoothed nor deliberately exaggerated imperfections — the goal is "realistic but beautiful"
- **Realism does not mean flatness** — Photorealistic ancient style emphasizes a genuinely photographable visual expression (real lighting positions, real materials, real movement rhythm), amplifying emotional impact through shot composition and mood design rather than relying on spectacle effects

---

## 4. Ancient-Style Scene Spatial Elements

Scene elements unique to the ancient-style worldview and their visual narrative function:

- **Gauze curtains/screens/door frames** — A natural framing-composition device, creating a layered "can't see through" sense of spatial depth
- **Courtyard/flowering trees/rain curtain** — A natural carrier for negative-space composition; the scene is the emotion: a courtyard full of blooming flowers = release, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, falling leaves drifting = sorrow of parting
- **Candlelight/moonlight/window light** — The light-source carriers of the ancient-style world: candlelight = warm/intimate (Scheme C), moonlight = cold/lonely (Scheme D), window light = everyday/tranquil (Scheme E)
- **Use scenic cutaway shots to transition between sequences** — This style has a rich library of scene assets (variants across time of day/weather); use scenic cutaways as an emotional buffer between sequences instead of hard cuts
- **Use visuals rather than dialogue at turning points** — Prioritize visual means (sudden lighting change, jump cut in shot scale, cutaway metaphor) over dialogue explanation

## 5. Ancient-Style Instruments and Ambient Sound

Sound-element constraints within the ancient-style worldview:

### Instrument Choice

- **Xiao (vertical flute)** — The core instrument for desolate, lonely, sorrowful passages; best expresses a cold, mournful feel
- **Erhu** — Passages of emotional turmoil, grief, longing; its weeping, bowed-string quality suits emotional outbursts
- **Suona** — Passages of intense emotional swings (great sorrow, great joy, fateful turning points, climax); use sparingly, but it lands like a bombshell when used
- **Guqin** — Opening tone-setting / calm passages, paired with the xiao
- **Pipa** — An accent for tense, urgent passages; not the lead instrument
- A string bed can add cinematic feel but should not overpower the scene

### Instrument Combination Strategy

| Emotional Stage | Instrument Combination |
|---|---|
| Calm/opening/closing | Guqin solo, or guqin + xiao |
| Sorrow deepening | Xiao + erhu |
| Emotional outburst/fateful turning point | Suona solo, or suona + erhu |
| Tense/urgent | Pipa accent + string bed |

### Ancient-Style Ambient Sound

- **Typical ambient sound layers** — Cicadas and insects chirping / murmuring stream / wind through bamboo / marketplace hawking / night rain dripping on eaves / rustling fabric / soft windchime ringing
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds per scene** to aid subsequent sound design. The richer the ambient sound layering, the more immersive the ancient-style scene feels
